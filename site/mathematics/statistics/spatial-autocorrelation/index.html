Spatial Autocorrelation

Moran's I¶

Moran's I is a measure of spatial autocorrelation. It measures the degree to which a variable is correlated with itself in space. The formula for Moran's I is given by:

\[ I = \frac{N}{W} \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \sum_{j=1}^{N} w_{ij}(x_i - \bar{x})(x_j - \bar{x})}{\sum_{i=1}^{N} (x_i - \bar{x})^2} \]

LISA¶

\[ I_i = \frac{x_i - \bar{x}}{m_2} \sum_{j=1}^{N} w_{ij} (x_j - \bar{x}) \]

Standardized Z-score:

\[ I_i = z_i \sum_{j=1}^{N} w_{ij} z_j\ \]
\[ m_2 = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{N}\ \]
